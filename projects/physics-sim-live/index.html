About This Simulation

Welcome to the Multiplayer Physics Simulation! This is a live, collaborative physics engine where you and other players can interact with the same particle world in real-time.

  • Spawn Particles: Click or tap anywhere on the canvas to create particles with your configured settings
  • Customize: Adjust mass, radius, and velocity using the controls below
  • Multiplayer: All particles and player actions are synchronized across connected clients
  • Reset: Use the "Reset World" button to clear all particles
